Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Flara ("Flara," "we," "us") helps you track symptoms tied to chronic conditions like PCOS, endometriosis, and fibromyalgia, and turns your logs into AI-generated insights and doctor-ready reports. Because this involves health information, we've tried to be extra plain about what we collect and why. If anything here is unclear, reach out — see contact info below.

Information we collect

  • Account info: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo.
  • Health & symptom data you log: pain level, energy level, mood, sleep hours, cycle day, symptoms (e.g. bloating, headache, brain fog), any free-text notes you write, your self-identified condition, and cycle length.
  • Community content: if you post to the community feed, we store the text and, internally, which account it came from — even though other users see it as anonymous.

We don't currently run behavioral analytics or ad tracking on Flara.

How we use it

To run the core app: save your logs, calculate your trends and streaks.

AI insights: the numbers and notes from a log entry are sent to our AI provider, Groq, to generate the short personalized insight you see after logging. Groq processes this to return a response — it isn't used to train our own models.

To generate your doctor report: your PDF is built entirely in your browser from data already loaded there. It is not uploaded to or stored on our servers as part of generating it.

To operate community features, if you choose to use them.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising networks. We share data only with the providers that run Flara, each processing it solely to provide their service to us:

  • Google — sign-in
  • Supabase — database and backend hosting
  • Groq — AI processing for insights
  • Vercel — web hosting

Data security

Your data is protected with row-level security in our database, so only your account can read or write your own logs, and connections to Flara are encrypted. No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your choices & rights

You can ask to access, export, or delete your data at any time by contacting us at flara.support@gmail.com. Deleting your account removes your logs and profile from our active database.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active, or until you ask us to delete it.

Community posts

Posts are shown to other users without your name attached. Please avoid including identifying details you wouldn't want associated with your account internally.

Children's privacy

Flara is intended for adults. It is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect data from, anyone under 18.

International users

Our service providers may process data in different countries. If you're located in India, we aim to handle your personal data — including sensitive personal data — consistent with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. If you're in the EU/UK or another jurisdiction with its own data protection law, the same principles of purpose limitation and data minimization guide how we handle your information.

Not medical advice

Flara is a self-tracking tool. Nothing in the app, including AI-generated insights, is a medical diagnosis or a substitute for care from a qualified healthcare provider.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Flara changes. We'll update the date at the top when we do.
